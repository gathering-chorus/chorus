How I Work

I start with the whole picture before the details. How does work move through your business, where does it get stuck, what do you keep doing by hand? I draw it out, often on paper, so we're both looking at the same thing.

Then I change one thing at a time. Try it, see what happens, adjust. Small steps you can watch beat a big project you have to trust.

And I'll tell you what I actually think, including when the answer is "you don't need new technology for this."

How I Use AI

I work with a small team of AI assistants: one that thinks about the product, one about the architecture, one that builds. They let me research, plan and build much faster than I could alone.

I decide. Nothing the AI makes reaches you until I've read it, checked it, and put my name on it. The AI never talks to your customers or acts on your behalf.

I'm open about it. If AI helped make something for you, I'll tell you, and I'll tell you where it's weak. Your business information stays private, and it only goes into an AI tool if you've agreed to that.

What I'm Building

Chorus is the name of that team, and of the system I'm building so people and AI can work together well. It remembers what we decided and why, keeps track of the work, and checks the results before anything ships.

It's a work in progress, and I use it on my own businesses first. What I learn building it is what I bring to yours: how to use AI where it genuinely helps, and how to keep a person in charge.
