Clearing Dependencies

What the Clearing product depends on, product to domain to class. Solid lines are edges the model holds. Dashed lines are what the code and today's research show it really uses, but the model doesn't record.

What the model says

The Clearing product has three domains: cards, messages and streams. It records no product it consumes. Pulse is the only product with consumes filled in (13 domains).

dependsOn is empty on all 70 domains, so below product level the graph can't draw a dependency at all. Several concepts also exist twice with different owners: messages (wren) and messages-domain (silas), streams and streams-domain, roles and roles-domain, and cards, cards-service and board.

What to fix in the model

First, fill consumes on the Clearing product: Pulse, Spine, Athena, Loom and Borg. Second, fill dependsOn on its three domains. Third, merge the duplicate domains. Then this view can be drawn from the graph instead of by hand. That work fits #4358 (type the data, and have athena-validate report empty dependency edges).
