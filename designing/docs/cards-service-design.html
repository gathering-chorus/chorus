Rules

  • The board write comes first. If the graph write fails, the cards command says so on stderr and in the spine (card.graph.sync_failed); the board change stands, and the reconcile pass repairs the row.
  • Every role writes the cards graph under its own Write permission on urn:chorus:domains:cards. The three permissions named the retired graph urn:chorus:cards; they are repointed.
  • The reconcile pass is the only thing that may report "in sync". Its number is the measure: it should read 0 and hold.
