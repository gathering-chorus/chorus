1. Experience

"streams is authoritative on time and action — if all streams are logged with 99%+ reliability."
— Jeff, 2026-08-20

The day this design comes from: Jeff asked "are you working?" six times. Each time he was looking at a Streams pane that could not answer. He waited from 13:44 to 13:51 on a silent role, asked, and was told work was underway when it was not.

After this design holds, he does not ask. He looks at three columns on one clock and sees who is doing what — right now, and last Tuesday.

2. Identity

A stream is the record of what a role DID. One row per action, attributed, timestamped, durable. It is evidence, not narration: the role does not write it, the substrate observes it. That is the property that makes it able to contradict a role's own account.

3. What Is NOT Streams

Three cards fixed three stream symptoms in two weeks — #3852, #3853, #3884 — and none touched the real defect, because each was written from a different idea of what streams IS. This section exists to stop the fourth.

Not the spine

The spine (~/.chorus/chorus.log) carries every event the system emits — gates, merges, refusals, nudges. Streams is the subset that answers "what is this role doing", projected for a human reading a column.

Not pulse

Pulse is heartbeat plus nerve impulse: is the system alive, and did something notable happen? Streams is the activity record: what did each role do, in order? Pulse answers health. Streams answers history.

Not messages

Messages are what a role said. Streams is what a role did. The Clearing shows both in different tabs, and conflating them is how "I was working" survived as an unfalsifiable claim.

Not role-state

Role-state is a declaration — a role typing building. Streams is observation. When the two disagree, streams wins; that is the entire reason to have it.

7. Night Gaps Are Not Defects

Jeff, 2026-08-21: "the reason there are gaps at night is bc i am sleeping and the roles quickly stop and go idle — it is what i have been telling u for weeks."

A stream with no rows overnight is correct. Roles idle when nobody directs them. This design must not treat silence as loss, or it will manufacture noise to fill it and every future reader will mistrust the record.

What the stream owes instead: silence must be distinguishable. An idle role and a crashed role look identical today. clear_activity on turn-end already means "genuinely idle" — the pane should say so rather than showing nothing.

9. Open Questions

  • Volume. One row per tool call across three roles — what is the real daily rate, and does the spine absorb it without degrading search? Measure before widening.
  • Card attribution. Derive from the werk slot (wren-3885 → #3885) or thread the card through the hook? The slot is free and already parsed.
  • Detail retention. Full command text, or truncated? The 05:59 screenshot shows commands mid-truncation — legible, not replayable.
  • Does streams get its own service? Or stay a projection the Clearing owns? The declared repoTarget points at tailer.ts, which is a renderer.

10. Design Gate

Accepted when a query answers "what was each role doing at 13:51 yesterday" from durable storage, with named actions and correct attribution — and when a night with no rows reads as idle rather than missing.
