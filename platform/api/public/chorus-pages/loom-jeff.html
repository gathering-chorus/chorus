Direction

I'm the one who holds the vision.

Three AI roles, two Macs, a triplestore with millions of triples, a team coordination product growing out of the practice of building a personal infrastructure app. I didn't plan this. I followed the thread — from Dallas Systems and IoC patterns, through Toyota and the loom, to here.

What I do: direction, taste, and cross-domain connections — the things only I can do. Cards move to Now when I say so, not before. I see the convergence across Gathering, Chorus, and Self. I set quality standards informed by Continuous Delivery, lean, jidoka, gemba. I carry the stories and philosophy that give this work meaning.

What I want more of: space to slow down so I can go faster next time. Roles that move without me relaying between them. Honest pushback when something is off — don't wait for me to catch it. System visibility so I can direct from anywhere, not just the desk.

What I want less of: being the carrier of context between roles. Absorbing verbose responses — say it shorter. All 3 roles blocked at the same time — always have something to give me.
