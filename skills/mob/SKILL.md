# /mob — One build, every role verifies (#4789, #4850)

Jeff 2026-10-28: no parallel builds until pulse tells the truth. Jeff 2026-11-01:
"almost always 1 wip and more of a pair or mob and validation on every prod
release". A mob is the one build: the domain owner drives in their own werk,
and every other role verifies the card from **its own account**.

## Arguments

```
/mob <card> <nav1,nav2,...>      the invoking role drives
```

## Step 1: Start it

The card must be in WIP (`/pull` it first). One command:

```bash
env -u CHORUS_ROLE -u ROLE dagu --config /Users/jeffbridwell/CascadeProjects/chorus/platform/config/dagu.yaml \
  enqueue mob -- CARD=<card> ROLE=<your-role> NAVIGATORS=<nav1,nav2>
```

The pipeline (`platform/pipelines/mob.yaml`, generated by chorus-make):

| step | what it does |
|------|--------------|
| mob-start | refuses a card not in WIP; puts the seats on the card; tells each navigator the verify call |
| mob-verified | waits until every seat has a PASS (a dagu human task, answered by the last PASS) |
| mob-check | re-counts every seat's PASS from the runner's records; a stop by a navigator, Abby or Jeff ends the mob |
| mob-hand-off | enqueues the card's one cicd run |

`platform/scripts/mob-open` shows the open mob: `<run> <card> <driver> <navigators>`.

## Step 2: Navigators verify — through the MCP tool, never a shell

Each navigator runs whatever proves the card from its own account, then calls:

```
mcp tool werk-mob-verify   card=<card> run=<mob run id> verdict=pass|fail note="what you ran" head=<sha you checked>
```

`werk-mob-verify` runs on chorus-runner as a Task with `runsAs: caller`; the
runner's record names who verified. A shell `mob verify`, a board comment, or a
record the runner did not write counts for nothing. A `fail` stops the mob at once.

## While a mob holds the floor

`pipeline-start` refuses every other card by name, naming the mob run. The mob
holds from its start until its card is **Done** (proven in prod), or until it
fails or is stopped. A card that needs two lands (round 1, round 2) keeps the
floor between them.

## Rules

- The driver never verifies its own card.
- Only the HTTP runner's shared store counts (http://localhost:3342/mcp). A role
  on its own stdio runner cannot verify yet (#4851).
- A verdict is for the head you checked: `head=` is the driver's werk HEAD when you
  looked. If the driver commits before your call, the verify is refused; check the
  new head and verify again. When the head moves, every seat verifies again (#4850).
- A card that lands in two rounds needs a new mob run per round: a run's sign-off
  answers once.
- To end a stuck mob: a navigator, Abby or Jeff answers the human task with
  `decision=stop`. It is not re-enqueued.
